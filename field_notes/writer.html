Engineering practice accumulates wisdom through repeated encounters with failure, recovery, and adaptation. The challenge is not collecting lessons learned but encoding them into formats that survive organizational turnover and toolchain evolution. The JITC Meta-Framework addresses this by treating engineering wisdom as structured data with explicitly separated provenance layers.
